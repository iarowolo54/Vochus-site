# Em-dash replacements

Every em dash (—) on the site was replaced with punctuation that fits the sentence: a full stop where the second half is a complete thought, a colon where it introduces or explains, a comma where it is an aside, and a middle dot (·) where it separated parts of a label (plan · amount × duration). No en dashes (–) were in use; number ranges would keep them.

Checked: page copy, UI chrome (ledger labels, buttons, status messages), `<title>`s, meta descriptions, alt text, the footer tagline and the ledger script. None remained.

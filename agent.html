Important information

12% p.a. interest: per annum · online savers only. Interest is calculated on your total savings at the end of your cycle, prorated for the months you saved, and added to your payout automatically. Applies to online contributions via the Customer Portal. Ask your agent about eligibility.

Group Savings: Community payout. Everyone contributes the same amount each cycle. One person receives the full pool, rotating until every member has been paid out.

Android only. Allow installs from your browser when Android asks. Each app is about 46 MB.

Never share your password or one-time codes with anyone, including Vochus staff or agents.

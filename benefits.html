Why Vochus

Built around how you save

Every feature of Vochus Easy Save was designed for the way Nigerians earn, live, and build wealth.

12% Annual Interest

Earn while you save. Online savers receive 12% per annum on their total balance, added automatically to every payout. No forms, no separate claim.

Safe & BVN Verified

Your identity is verified with BVN and a valid government ID. Every contribution is recorded digitally. No cash goes missing, no disputed payments.

Full Payout, Zero Deductions

Every naira you save comes back to you at cycle end, plus your interest. Vochus earns from agent commissions, never from cutting your savings balance.

100% Online

Register, contribute, track your balance and claim your payout entirely from your phone. No queues, no office visits, no paperwork. Ever.

Group Savings (Ajo/Esusu)

Traditional community savings, fully digitised. Transparent cycle timelines, digital records, no cash confusion. Save together, win together.

Your Own Agent

Every saver has a dedicated Vochus agent: your local support contact for registration, contributions, payout questions, and everything in between.

Real-Time Tracking

Your Customer Portal shows your current balance, contribution history, and upcoming payout date at any time. No need to call anyone to check.

Builds Financial Discipline

A fixed savings cycle creates the habit. Most Vochus savers re-enrol immediately after their first payout. Once you see the process work, it's hard to stop.

Re-enrol Any Time

Once your cycle ends and you're paid out, start a new cycle immediately with the same plan or switch to a different one. No waiting period, no new paperwork.

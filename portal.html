How to get started

  • New saver: choose Create an Account, enter your phone number, ID and BVN, then pick a plan. Your agent will confirm your account.
  • Existing saver: choose Log In and sign in with your registered phone number and password.
  • Forgot your password? Use the reset link on the login page, or ask your agent to help.

Need help?

Browse the FAQs or contact us. Never share your password or one-time codes with anyone, including Vochus staff or agents.

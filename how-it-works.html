Simple for every kind of customer

Save online, save through an agent with cash, or send a gift card. Here is how each one works.

  1. 1Easy Save

    Save online

    1. RegisterOpen your account on the Customer Portal with your phone number, a valid ID and your BVN.
    2. PayChoose daily, weekly or monthly savings and pay by bank transfer, USSD or debit card.
    3. Track and withdrawYour balance updates in real time. At the end of your cycle, receive your savings plus 12% annual interest.
  2. 2Easy Save

    Save with an agent (cash)

    1. Meet your agentA registered Vochus agent enrols you and sets up your daily or weekly savings cycle.
    2. Hand over your cashThe agent collects your cash in person and records the deposit.
    3. Your account is creditedThe agent credits your Vochus account from their own funded agent account, and your balance updates.
  3. 3Gift Cards

    Send a gift card

    1. Choose your cardPick a physical or digital gift card, or ask for a custom design for your business.
    2. Pay securelyPay online. Your card is created and recorded in our card registry.
    3. Gift and redeemGive the card to your recipient. They redeem it through the Vochus system.

Group Savings: Community payout. Everyone contributes the same amount each cycle. One person receives the full pool, rotating until every member has been paid out.

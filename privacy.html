Privacy Policy

How Vochus Global Services Ltd collects, uses and protects your personal data.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register or use Vochus Easy Save, we collect: your full name, phone number, email address, Bank Verification Number (BVN), bank account details for payouts, and device/browser data for security purposes. We do not collect payment card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your savings account
  • To process contributions and disburse payouts to your registered bank account
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraud (BVN is used solely for this purpose)
  • To assign a dedicated Vochus agent to your account
  • To send you account statements, payout notifications, and important service updates
  • To comply with Nigerian financial regulations

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share your information only with: our regulated banking partners (for fund custody and payout processing), identity-verification services (for BVN validation), and Vochus agents assigned to your account. All third-party partners are contractually bound to protect your data.

4. Data Security

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Customer funds are held in ring-fenced, dedicated bank accounts separate from Vochus operational accounts. We apply role-based access controls internally — only authorised personnel can access account data, and all access is logged.

5. Cookies and Tracking

The Vochus website uses essential session cookies to maintain your login state and a small number of analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. We do not use cross-site advertising trackers. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; this will not affect your ability to save or receive payouts.

6. Your Rights

Under Nigerian data-protection law (NDPR), you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to regulatory retention requirements), and withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time.

7. Retention

We retain account and transaction records for a minimum of seven years as required by Nigerian financial regulations. After account closure, personal data is anonymised or deleted in accordance with our data retention schedule, except where longer retention is legally required.

8. Contact for Privacy Matters

To exercise any of your rights or to raise a privacy concern, contact our Data Protection Officer at info@vochuseasysave.com with the subject line "Data Privacy Request." We will respond within 14 business days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or a notice in your Customer Portal at least 14 days before taking effect. Continued use of Vochus Easy Save after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

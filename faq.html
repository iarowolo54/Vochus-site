FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about saving with Vochus.

For savers
How do I register with Vochus Easy Save? +
Visit vochuseasysave.com and click Create an Account on the Log In page. Fill in your name, phone number, BVN and preferred savings plan. Your dedicated Vochus agent will contact you within 24 hours to complete onboarding — no branch visits required.
What savings plans are available? +
We offer four flexible plans: Daily (save every day, paid out after your cycle), Weekly (weekly contributions, steady growth), Monthly (larger monthly deposits, best for salary earners), and Group / Ajo (traditional communal savings, fully digitalised). All plans earn 12% annual interest.
How is the 12% annual interest calculated? +
Interest accrues daily on your saved balance at the equivalent daily rate of 12% per annum. For example, ₦50,000 saved for a full year earns ₦6,000 in interest. The exact amount is shown in your Customer Portal at any time and is paid out with your principal at the end of the cycle.
When and how am I paid out? +
Your payout — principal plus interest — is sent directly to your registered bank account when your savings cycle ends. The payout date is visible in your Customer Portal. Funds typically arrive within one business day. You can choose to re-enrol immediately into a new cycle after payout.
Is my money safe with Vochus? +
Vochus Global Services Ltd is a registered Nigerian company. All customer funds are held in dedicated, ring-fenced bank accounts separate from operational funds. Your BVN is used solely for identity verification and is never shared with third parties. We employ bank-grade encryption on all data transmissions.
Can I withdraw early or pause my contributions? +
Savings plans are designed as fixed cycles to help you maintain discipline and earn the full interest. Early withdrawals may forfeit accrued interest. If you encounter an emergency, contact your dedicated Vochus agent — they can advise you on your options based on your specific plan and contribution history.
What is the Group Savings (Ajo/Esusu) plan? +
The Group plan is a digitalised version of the traditional Ajo or Esusu rotating savings system. Each member contributes a fixed amount on a set schedule, and members receive the pooled total in rotation. Vochus manages the cycle digitally — transparent timelines, digital records, no cash confusion, and the same 12% interest on individual contributions.
How do I become a Vochus agent? +
Apply via vochuseasysave.com/agent. Successful agents receive a starter pack (T-shirt, cap, bag, power bank, Bluetooth printer and paper rolls) plus full training and digital tools. You earn 65% of every commission generated by savers in your portfolio — with no cap on earnings.
For agents: Getting started
Do I need a smartphone to be an agent? +
Yes. You need a capable smartphone to use the Agent Portal, record contributions, print receipts over Bluetooth and show customers their savings progress in the field.
What does Vochus give me when I join? +
Every agent receives a starter kit at onboarding: a branded T-shirt, face cap, cross bag, power bank, Bluetooth printer and thermal paper rolls. Keep the printer and paper rolls stocked, because every offline customer must get a printed receipt for each cash contribution.
Do customers have to download an app? +
No. Online customers use the Customer Portal in any phone browser. Offline customers need nothing at all, because their agent handles everything for them on the Agent Portal.
Which ID does a customer need? +
Offline customers give their agent a valid ID: national ID, driver's licence, voter's card or passport. Online customers upload a valid ID or enter their BVN to verify their identity.
For agents: Registration
What is the difference between online and offline customers? +
Online customers register themselves through their agent's link or QR code and pay digitally. Offline customers are registered by the agent on the Agent Portal and pay the agent in cash. After enrolment the savings cycle is the same for both. The only difference is who records the contribution and who submits the token.
How do I get customers to register online under me? +
Share your Customer Portal link or hand out a QR-code flyer from your Promo Materials. Anyone who scans it and registers appears in your Agent Portal member list automatically.
What if I have no internet when I meet a customer? +
Use the paper registration form to capture their details, then transfer everything to the Agent Portal as soon as you are back online.
What does an offline customer receive after registering? +
The system gives them a unique Customer ID, and you print their QR-coded membership card from the Agent Portal. The card is their physical proof of enrolment. Encourage them to save the Customer Portal link as well.
Can an offline customer become an online payer later? +
Yes, many do once they see the portal. Pull up the Customer Portal on your phone and show them their balance. Please confirm the steps with your supervisor before moving a customer.
For agents: Contributions & payouts
How do I record a cash contribution? +
Collect the cash, open the Agent Portal, select the customer and click Record Contribution. Then print the receipt or send it by SMS. Do this for every offline customer in every cycle.
What happens if I forget to record a contribution? +
Unrecorded payments cannot be paid out. In a group, cash contributions cannot be added after you click Process Cycle. Record each payment straight away.
Do I have to give a receipt every time? +
Yes, for every cash payment. It protects you and the customer, and every offline customer must receive one.
When can I click Process Cycle? +
Only after every contribution for that cycle is in: online payments confirmed on the portal and offline cash payments recorded.
What is the redemption token and how long does it last? +
After you process a cycle the system generates a single-use redemption token. Online customers see it on their portal and submit it themselves. For offline customers, you tell them in person or by call, and you submit it for them on the Agent Portal. A token expires in 48 hours if it is not submitted.
What if a token expires before it is submitted? +
Contact your supervisor straight away. To avoid this, tell offline customers as soon as their token is ready.
How does the customer get their money? +
Once the token is submitted, you open the approval screen in the Agent Portal and click Approve & Pay. Funds are then released for online customers. For offline customers, you count the cash and hand it to them.
For agents: Group savings (Ajo / Esusu)
How big can a group be? +
The golden rule: group size must always equal the number of cycles. A group of 5 people runs for exactly 5 cycles. Every member contributes every cycle but redeems only once.
Can one group have both online and offline members? +
Yes. Online members join with the group join code. Offline members are added by the agent manually. Both types appear in the same cycle timeline on the Agent Portal.
How do I create and start a group? +
Create the group on the Agent Portal by setting the type, amount, duration and size. The portal gives you a join code such as GRP-A4X2. Add your members, then when the group is full set who redeems in which cycle and click Activate.
How do members join a group? +
Online members enter the join code on their portal, review the group details and click Accept & Join. For offline members, open the group and use Add Member Manually with the customer's ID. They do not need portal access.
For agents: Earning commission
How much commission do I earn? +
You earn 65% of the commission on every contribution, and Vochus keeps 35%. The rate is the same whether the customer paid online or you collected cash.
Do online customers' contributions earn me commission too? +
Yes, at the same 65% rate. Customers who register through your link or QR code are in your book, so their contributions count for you.
How can I increase my earnings? +
Enrol more customers and run more groups. A group of 10 members gives you 10 contributions per cycle from one setup. Customers who finish a cycle and re-enrol give you recurring commission, and customers who share your portal link grow your book without extra effort.
How and when is my commission paid? +
Your Agent Portal tracks your earnings in real time. Please ask your supervisor for the current commission payment schedule.
For agents: Help & support
A customer asks about interest or returns. What do I say? +
Only quote what Vochus has published at vochuseasysave.com or what your supervisor has confirmed. Never promise returns that have not been published.
Who do I contact for help? +
Your Vochus supervisor. They can help with onboarding support, commission schedules and paper registration forms. Both online and offline workflows are fully supported.
